# About Me

I build software, experiment with technology, and work on projects that interest me.

I sometimes use AI as a programming and development tool, but I believe its use should always be transparent.

## AI Transparency

If AI was used to create or significantly contribute to one of my projects, repositories, or other works, **I will clearly mark it as AI-assisted or AI-generated**.

If something I publish is **not marked as AI-assisted**, then AI did not contribute to its creation.

I want people to know what was made by me and what was created with the help of AI.

## My stance on AI

While I use AI as a programming tool, I strongly oppose using AI to copy, imitate, or take credit for other people's projects, artwork, or creative work.

I am personally against the generation of AI-created images and artwork, especially when it replaces, imitates, or undermines the work of human artists.

Whenever possible, I prefer to support **human artists and creators** and respect the time, skill, and creativity behind their work.

**AI should be a tool — not an excuse to take someone else's work.**
